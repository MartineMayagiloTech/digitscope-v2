How it works

Data: public Deriv WebSocket (app_id 1089), no token, read-only ticks. Digit = last character of the quote formatted to the market’s own pip_size. Duplicate epochs are ignored; reconnects use backoff and reload history. Stats are descriptive: intervals are 95% Wilson. Deriv’s synthetic indices are designed so each tick is independent, so no score here is a forecast. Deviations across 10 digits are expected by chance alone.
